About

Sarvixa Labs is a clinical AI research group. We study clinical problems from their foundations, solve them from first principles, and examine every solution through the lens of trustworthiness.

Our work currently spans medical imaging, medical signals and electronic health records (EHR). Our current applications include oncology, neurology and radiology.

We aim to extend our research to multi-omics, precision medicine and longevity research, with a focus on multimodal and foundation models that learn from many kinds of clinical data together.

01

In the long term, take validated research toward clinical trials and real use.
